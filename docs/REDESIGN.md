# Full visual redesign and animated menu

## Direction
An intimate editorial coaching journal: warm cream, soft lavender, deeper plum, purposeful serif typography and an original line-drawn branch as a quiet sign of growth. Retain the original Esperanza logo and client photography. Make the entire experience feel related while giving each page its own composition.

## Scope
- Home: lavender split hero with large photo, editorial introduction, service rows, story film and FAQs.
- About: opening spread, portrait and three-part biography, philosophy and consultation invitation.
- Services: linked chapter index, three full-width service chapters and eligibility list.
- Why Esperanza: centered values opening and numbered narrative beside a portrait.
- Reviews: intentional, clearly pending storytelling space; no invented testimonials.
- Contact: introduction, personal-contact sidebar, independent form, booking/community information.
- Guide: explicitly labelled cover concept, language options and independent request interface.
- Privacy: overview, anchored contents and readable disclosure chapters.
- 404 and legacy routes follow the new shared system.
- Shared footer, CTAs, form panels, FAQ and header rebuilt consistently.

## Menu sequence
The two-line icon morphs into a close icon; a native full-screen dialog enters over 420 ms with link entrances staggered 45 ms apart. Closing reverses the panel and restores focus after 440 ms. Escape and the close button use the same path. Native modality contains focus and makes background content inert. Body scrolling is locked while open. The menu itself scrolls on short screens. Reduced motion removes transitions and closes immediately. A no-JavaScript navigation list remains available. Links remain normal local navigation.

## Retained limitations
No new clinical content, credential, testimonial or medical outcome is added. The original integration and privacy approvals, guide files and video captions remain pending. No deployment is included.
